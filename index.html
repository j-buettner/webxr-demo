The Great Acceleration, walk-in

Four indicators from 1900 to 2020. Every bar is scaled to the reference year, which stands at 100%. Click any bar to make its year the reference. Each row has a small lit door on the side of its 2020 bar: click it to step into that indicator's room.

On screen: drag or swipe sideways to look around · shift-drag or right-drag to pan · use the move pad or W/A/S/D and arrow keys to walk.

In VR: trigger or pinch on a bar to rebase, or on a door to enter · grip (or pinch empty space) to grab and turn the chart · both grips to scale it · left stick to walk, right stick to turn · B or Y to reset or leave a room.
